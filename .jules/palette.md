## 2025-04-26 - Missing A11y Bindings in Complex Forms
**Learning:** I discovered a widespread pattern across forms (e.g., `profile-form.tsx`) where labels were entirely disconnected from their corresponding input elements due to missing `htmlFor` and `id` attributes. Additionally, validation error messages (like `FieldError`) lacked `role="alert"` attributes, and were not connected back to their inputs via `aria-describedby`, making the forms very difficult for screen readers to interpret correctly.
**Action:** When adding or updating forms and form fields in this codebase, explicitly map `<label htmlFor="id">` to `<Input id="id">`. Furthermore, ensure validation error components receive `role="alert"` and use `aria-describedby` on the input element pointing to the error message's ID to maintain strict accessibility standards.
