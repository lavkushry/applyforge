## 2026-04-19 - Missing Form Label Bindings
**Learning:** Widespread accessibility issues exist across `apps/web/components/forms/` components (e.g., `role-create-form.tsx`, `job-create-form.tsx`, `auth-form.tsx`). Labels are missing `htmlFor` attributes, inputs are missing `id`s, and validation errors are not linked to inputs via `aria-describedby` and `role="alert"`. This prevents screen readers from announcing the field purpose and errors correctly, and users cannot click the label to focus the input.
**Action:** When creating or modifying forms, always ensure labels are bound to inputs using matching `htmlFor` and `id` props. If there's an error message, ensure it has an `id` and `role="alert"`, and add `aria-describedby` to the corresponding input linking to the error's `id`.
