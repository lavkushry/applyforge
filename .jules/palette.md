## 2024-04-14 - Widespread Missing Form Bindings
**Learning:** Forms within `apps/web/components/forms/` (e.g., `role-create-form.tsx`, `job-create-form.tsx`, `profile-form.tsx`, `settings-form.tsx`, `auth-form.tsx`) currently have widespread accessibility issues. They consistently lack `htmlFor`/`id` bindings connecting labels to inputs, and are missing `aria-describedby` linking inputs to validation errors equipped with `role="alert"`. This makes the forms extremely difficult to use with screen readers as inputs lack accessible names and errors lack screen-reader association.
**Action:** Always verify `htmlFor` and `id` pairings on `<label>` and `<Input>` tags when creating or editing form components. For validation errors, ensure the error message has an `id` and `role="alert"`, and the corresponding `<Input>` uses `aria-describedby` pointing to that `id`, along with `aria-invalid` if an error is present.
