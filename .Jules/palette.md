## 2026-04-08 - Missing Form Label Bindings
**Learning:** Found widespread missing form label bindings across components (missing `htmlFor` and `id` mapping) and lack of error-to-input associations for screen readers (missing `aria-invalid`, `aria-describedby`, and `role="alert"`).
**Action:** Always verify label bindings and error associations when working on forms to ensure proper accessibility for screen readers.
